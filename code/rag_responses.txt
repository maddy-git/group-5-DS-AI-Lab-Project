Response: The Clothing Retail return policy allows you to return or exchange eligible items within the specified return/exchange period mentioned on the product detail page.

**Eligibility for Return:**
You can initiate a return or exchange request if:
*   The product is damaged, defective, or of poor quality.
*   The wrong product or wrong size/color was delivered.
*   The product shows manufacturing defects.

**Return Conditions:**
*   Returned products must be unused, unwashed, unaltered, and in their original condition with all tags, invoices, and packaging intact.
*   Products received with missing accessories, altered conditions, or without original packaging may be rejected during quality check.
*   All accessories or bundled items (if applicable) must be returned together.

**Non-Returnable Products:**
Certain categories are non-returnable, such as innerwear, accessories (like gloves, masks, and precious jewelry), and electronics/gadgets marked "NonReturnable." Refunds for these will only be approved if the product was:
*   Defective
*   Damaged in transit
*   Incorrectly shipped

**Return Process:**
1.  Log in to your Clothing Retail account.
2.  Go to “My Orders” and click on Return/Exchange for the respective item.
3.  Choose Return or Exchange, specify the reason, and upload supporting images (if damaged).
4.  Select your preferred refund mode (original payment method or Clothing Retail Wallet).
5.  A pickup will be scheduled within 4–7 days, or instructions for self-shipping will be provided.

**Cost and Pickup:**
*   Returns or exchanges are accepted free of cost in most locations where pickup service is available.
*   If pickup is unavailable at your location, you may self-ship the product to their warehouse, and the shipping cost will be reimbursed as Clothing Retail credits.
